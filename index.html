FAQ

Useful answers.

What do you manage for landlords?

Our service covers the practical tenancy lifecycle, including marketing and tenant setup, referencing, tenancy administration, rent collection, ongoing communication, inspections, renewals and coordinating repairs and maintenance.

How are repairs handled?

Tenants report issues to Home4Letting and we coordinate the next steps. Where landlord approval is required, we contact the landlord before arranging the relevant works.

Do you have your own contractors?

Home4Letting is connected to Kamran Construction, an established construction business owned by the same family. This allows us to support landlords with everything from routine repairs to refurbishment and larger works.

Can tenants redecorate or alter a property?

Tenants should obtain permission before carrying out decorating or alterations. The answer can depend on the proposed change and the tenancy agreement.
